# Exercise 05: Demonstrate features to a customer

Now that you've configured the feature to meet our needs, you can demonstrate its functionality to the customer. Typically, the intent and the Customer Intent Agent are part of a larger, overarching demo.

## Talk track

Imagine that a customer in Brazil has contacted us because their machine no longer brews coffee as intended. After some self-service troubleshooting, the case is escalated to a representative who assists the customer in troubleshooting and resolving the issue.

### Business value statements:

These statements represent themes that should be part of your narrative. As you demonstrate the
functionality to your customer, return to these themes and reiterate them throughout your demo.

- Improve customer satisfaction with faster, more accurate responses
- Streamline case resolution with intelligent case management and AI assistance

### Items to highlight during the demo

- Emphasize how **Copilot** reduces agent effort with real-time suggestions
- Highlight how **Customer Intent Agent** identifies why the customer is reaching out
- Show how representatives can use instructions to help capture data
- Demonstrate how solutions can be attached to specific intents to reduce the time required to resolve
	an issue

> [!WARNING]
> This Customer Intent Agent demo talk track is part of a larger overall demonstration. Typically, the Case Management Agent automatically creates the case, and the Knowledge Management Agent helps to automatically create articles. These two talk tracks are covered in future labs: **Configure the Case Management Agent** and **Configure the Knowledge Management Agent**.
